# Document 01 --- Project Vision

## Mission

Lubanda is **not** a genealogy drawing application.

Lubanda is a **Natural Genealogy Growth Platform** that simulates the
organic growth of a living tree while preserving perfect genealogical
correctness.

### Golden Rule

> Never draw a tree. Grow it.

### Core Goals

-   Perfect genealogy.
-   Organic growth.
-   No branch intersections.
-   No label overlap.
-   Readable at every zoom level.
-   Scalable from small families to 1,500+ people.
-   Separation between logic and artistic rendering.

# Document 03 --- Natural Growth Rules

## Philosophy

Branches are living structures.

They adapt.

They search for space.

They never search for fixed directions.

### Growth Rules

-   No radial symmetry.
-   No mirrored branches.
-   No predefined angles.
-   Upward growth is optional.
-   Downward growth is allowed.
-   Side growth is encouraged.
-   Branches remember previous direction.
-   Curves must be smooth.
-   Small edits should produce small changes.
-   Beauty comes from natural growth.

# Document 04 --- AI Responsibilities

## AI May

-   Analyze reference images.
-   Extract growth philosophy.
-   Suggest organic trajectories.
-   Review visual quality.
-   Generate bark.
-   Assist administrators.

## AI Must Never

-   Change genealogy.
-   Move labels independently.
-   Redesign approved skeleton.
-   Override engine decisions.

### Golden Rule

AI suggests.

Lubanda decides.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Document 05 --- Engineering Standards

## Mandatory Acceptance

-   Correct genealogy.
-   No duplicate IDs.
-   No missing parents.
-   No cycles.
-   No branch intersections.
-   No label overlap.
-   Stable growth.
-   Organic appearance.
-   Readable output.
-   SVG / PNG / PDF export.

### Performance Target

Support at least **1,500 people**.

# Document 06 --- Design Philosophy

## Decision Priority

1.  Genealogical correctness
2.  Readability
3.  Collision avoidance
4.  Organic growth
5.  Space utilization
6.  Beauty

### Rule Zero

If the generated tree looks computer-generated instead of naturally
grown, the implementation has failed.

### Reference Image Principle

Reference images are sources of **growth philosophy**, not templates to
copy.

Analyze.

Extract rules.

Never copy.
